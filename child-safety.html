Child Safety Standards

Swinglet is committed to maintaining a safe and respectful environment. We have a zero-tolerance policy for any form of child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

1. Age Restriction

Swinglet is strictly intended for individuals aged 18 years or older. We do not knowingly allow minors to create accounts or use the platform. Any account identified as underage will be immediately removed.

2. Zero Tolerance for CSAE

  • Any content involving minors in a sexual context
  • Sexual exploitation or abuse of children
  • Sharing, requesting, or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Any behavior that endangers minors

Any violation will result in immediate account suspension and may be reported to relevant law enforcement authorities.

3. Content Moderation

  • User reporting tools
  • Manual review via admin moderation
  • Immediate removal of violating content

4. Reporting Mechanism

Users can report inappropriate or harmful content directly within the app.

Contact us:
support@swinglet.app

5. Enforcement

  • Content will be removed immediately
  • Accounts will be permanently banned
  • Reports may be escalated to law enforcement authorities

6. Compliance

Swinglet complies with applicable child safety laws and regulations in India and internationally.
